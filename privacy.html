Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026. FRIDAY is a private assistant for its owner, operated through Telegram.

Google data accessed

With the owner's OAuth consent, FRIDAY uses the drive.file scope to create Google Sheets spreadsheets, read or update their cells, and list files created or opened through FRIDAY. The app does not request general access to all existing Drive files. Other enabled Google APIs, including Gmail and Calendar, are not connected under this authorization.

Use and sharing

Google file data is used to carry out the owner's requests. When a task requires reasoning about file contents, relevant data and instructions may be sent to the configured AI model provider (currently DeepSeek Direct or an OpenRouter fallback) to generate an answer or spreadsheet content. FRIDAY does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or generalized AI model training. Third-party providers process requests under their own terms and policies.

Storage and security

Spreadsheets remain in the owner's Google account. FRIDAY stores its OAuth refresh token in a restricted file on the owner's private server. Conversation messages and tool results are stored in the assistant's local SQLite history; older tool results may be pruned. Replies are sent to the Telegram chat or topic where the owner made the request. The app does not publish the token or Google file contents on this website.

Control and contact

The owner can revoke FRIDAY's Google access in their Google Account settings. Revocation stops future Google API access until consent is given again. To request removal of locally stored FRIDAY data or ask about this policy, contact moxhatishe@gmail.com.
