FRIDAY

FRIDAY is a private, single-user assistant operated through Telegram. It helps its owner find and read relevant Drive files and create documents or spreadsheets from them. FRIDAY is a separate personal project of this domain's owner, not a Neptun Service product.

Google access

FRIDAY requests the drive permission, which can access and manage all files in the owner's Google Drive. It searches folders and reads files relevant to a request, then creates documents, spreadsheets or folders as requested. It does not sync the whole Drive to its server. Google/Gemini models are not part of this connection.

FRIDAY is for its owner's personal use and is not open for public registration. It is not affiliated with Google.
